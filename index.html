About our practice

A family dental practice in Fremont

Lowell and Lorence Thomas Family Dentistry is a general dental practice on Warm Springs Boulevard in Fremont, California. Dr. Lowell Thomas, DMD, and the team care for patients of all ages — from a child’s first checkup to ongoing care for parents and grandparents.

Visits focus on the fundamentals of good oral health: regular exams, thorough cleanings, and honest, straightforward treatment when something needs attention. If you have a question about your care, call the office and the team will help you.

What we do

Services

General dental care for the whole family. Call the office to discuss your specific needs.

Checkups & Cleanings

Regular exams and professional cleanings to keep teeth and gums healthy and catch small problems early.

Fillings & Cavity Care

Treatment for cavities and tooth decay, so a small spot doesn’t turn into a bigger problem.

Gum Care

Care for gingivitis and periodontal (gum) disease — the foundation of a healthy smile.

Root Canal Care

Treatment for infected or painful teeth, including pulpitis and root canal infections.

Crowns & Tooth Repair

Repair for fractured or damaged teeth, restoring strength and comfort.

Tooth Replacement

Options for tooth loss and replacement — ask the office what fits your situation best.
